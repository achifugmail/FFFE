Fantasy Football Game Rules

Squad Composition

  • Your squad is selected via a draft process. Each user picks players in turn.
  • Each squad consists of a fixed number of players per position:
    • Goalkeepers (GK): usually 2
    • Defenders (DEF): e.g. 5
    • Wing Backs (WB): e.g. 2
    • Defensive Midfielders (DM): e.g. 2
    • Attacking Midfielders (AM): e.g. 4
    • Forwards (FW): e.g. 3
  • Exact numbers may be set by your league admin.
  • No two squads can own the same player at the same time.

Draft Process

  • The draft is turn-based. When it is your turn, you may select any available player.
  • Once a player is drafted, they are unavailable to other squads.
  • The draft continues until all squads are full.

Team Selection & Starting XI

You must select your team every gameweek – teams do not automatically carry over from the previous week.

All players in your selected team play each gameweek. There is no separate bench or starting lineup selection.

Position Constraints

When building your gameweek team, you must respect these limits:

  • Goalkeeper (GK): Maximum 1
  • Defense (DEF + WB + DM combined): Maximum 5 total
  • Attack (AM + FW combined): Maximum 5 total
  • Forwards (FW) alone: Maximum 3
  • Same Real-World Team Limit: Maximum 2 players from the same club in defensive positions (GK, DEF, WB, DM)

Team Management

  • You can add/remove players before the gameweek deadline
  • Changes are locked once the first game of the gameweek starts

Captain Selection

Each gameweek you must select one captain from your team.

  • Captain Multiplier: 1.5× points (all points your captain earns are multiplied by 1.5)
  • You can change your captain before the gameweek deadline
  • Only one player can be captain at a time; changing captain removes the status from your previous selection
  • Captain Limit: A player may be selected as captain no more than 5 times each half season

Substitutions

There are no automatic substitutions.

All players in your selected team play. If a player in your team doesn't play in real life, they score 0 points – no replacement occurs.

You can manually remove and replace players before the gameweek deadline.

Transfers

Transfer Limits

  • 20 standard transfers allowed per squad per draft period
  • Draft periods: gameweeks 1-19 OR gameweeks 20-38

Transfer Rules

  • Position matching required: The player you bring in must play the same position as the player going out
  • No duplicates: You cannot transfer in a player who is already owned by another squad in your league
  • Future team protection: You cannot transfer out a player who is selected in a future gameweek's team
  • Immediate processing: Transfers take effect instantly – no waiver period or claims system

All transfer activity is timestamped and tracked.

Competition Format & Winning

This is a head-to-head league with weekly placements and season-long points.

Each Gameweek

  • Teams compete for 1st place, 2nd place, and Last place within the league
  • Rankings are based on total points scored that gameweek
  • Ties are handled with fractional placement sharing
    • Example: If 3 teams tie for 1st place, each receives ⅓ of a 1st place credit

Season-Long Tracking

Your squad accumulates:

  • FirstPlaces count (can be fractional if tied)
  • SecondPlaces count (can be fractional if tied)
  • LastPlaces count (can be fractional if tied)
  • TotalPoints (sum of all gameweek scores)

Winner Determination

  • Gameweek winner: Highest points that week
  • Season winner: Highest TotalPoints across all gameweeks

Special Rules

  • In 2-team leagues, SecondPlaces is always set to 0
  • Gameweeks where all teams score ≤0 points are skipped in placement calculations

Scoring

Players earn points for your squad based on their real-life performances. The scoring rules are as follows:

Rule Points

Clean Sheet Requirements

A player earns clean sheet points if:

  • They play at least 30 minutes, AND
  • Their opponent scores 0 goals

Admin Adjustments

  • Admins can manually add or subtract points from any player's gameweek score
  • Adjustments include a comment field explaining the reason
  • All scores and placements are automatically recalculated after adjustments
  • Changes are logged in audit trails

Gameweek Deadlines

Deadline Time

Lineups lock when the first game of the gameweek starts.

After Deadline

  • No player changes allowed (add/remove/transfer)
  • No captain changes allowed

Before Deadline

  • Freely add/remove players (within position constraints)
  • Change your captain
  • Make transfers (within transfer limits)

Player Status Changes

Real-Life Transfers/Loans

  • Player data is synced with the Fantasy Premier League API
  • If a player transfers to a different club in real life, their stats continue to update
  • No automatic removal from squads required

Injured/Suspended Players

  • Players remain in your squad even if injured or suspended in real life
  • If they don't play, they score 0 points
  • You can transfer them out (subject to transfer limits) or keep them

Players Leaving the League

  • If a player leaves the Premier League entirely, they will likely stop accumulating stats
  • You may transfer them out (subject to transfer limits)

Tiebreakers

Gameweek Ties

  • Teams with identical gameweek scores share placements fractionally
  • Example: 3-way tie for 1st = each team gets 0.33 first places

Season Ties

If teams finish level on TotalPoints, the winner is determined by:

  1. Most FirstPlaces
  2. Most SecondPlaces
  3. Fewest LastPlaces
